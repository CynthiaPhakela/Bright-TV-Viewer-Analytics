# BrightTV Viewership Analytics – Business Intelligence Case Study

## Overview

This repository contains my end-to-end **BrightTV Viewership Analytics Case Study**, completed as part of the **BrightLearn Data Analytics Programme**. The project was designed to analyse customer viewing behaviour and provide data-driven recommendations to support BrightTV's strategic objective of **growing its subscription base**. Acting as a Business/Data Analyst, I transformed raw subscriber profile and viewing session data into meaningful business insights using **SQL in Databricks**, with the findings presented through interactive dashboards and executive-level visualisations. The project demonstrates the complete analytics lifecycle, from data cleaning and transformation to exploratory analysis, KPI development, dashboard creation, and strategic business recommendations. 

## Business Objective

BrightTV's CEO requested an analytical assessment of customer viewership patterns to help the Customer Value Management (CVM) team increase subscriber growth during the current financial year. The analysis focused on identifying user and content consumption trends, determining the factors influencing engagement, recommending content strategies to improve viewing on low-consumption days, and proposing initiatives to increase customer acquisition and retention. Additional insights beyond the original case study requirements were also explored to provide a more comprehensive understanding of customer behaviour.

## Project Workflow

The project followed a structured analytics process beginning with data exploration and quality assessment of the subscriber profile and viewership datasets. SQL was used extensively within **Databricks** to clean and standardise the data, resolve inconsistencies, create analytical views, engineer new features, calculate business KPIs, and prepare reporting datasets. The transformed data was then analysed to identify customer engagement patterns, viewing behaviour, subscriber demographics, channel performance, content preferences, and usage trends. The final insights were presented through interactive dashboards built in **Power BI** and **Google Data Studio**, supported by Excel-based validation and executive presentations designed in **Canva**.

The analysis provides actionable recommendations to help BrightTV improve subscriber engagement and support business growth.
